B2B COMMERCE

Connecting SMEs Through Business Social-Commerce

Gloqr is a business social-commerce platform designed to help small and medium-sized enterprises showcase their capabilities, manage sales opportunities, generate leads, and build meaningful business relationships.

CLIENT Gloqr
PLATFORM Web Platform
SERVICES USED Business Platform · Digital Experience · UX Strategy
INDUSTRY B2B Commerce
THE CHALLENGE

Helping SMEs Build Visibility and Grow Their Business

Small and medium-sized businesses often struggle to showcase their full capabilities online, respond to inquiries efficiently, manage leads, and establish meaningful business connections. Traditional business directories and fragmented sales processes can make it harder to turn business discovery into real opportunities.

OUR APPROACH

One Platform for Discovery, Sales, and Networking

Gloqr brings essential business growth tools together in one connected platform, helping businesses showcase their offerings, streamline sales activities, manage inquiries, and build a professional network.

Gloqr Shop

Showcase products, services, infrastructure, work culture, and management expertise.

Gloqr Salesman

Simplify quotation workflows and respond to potential customers with less manual effort.

Gloqr Mylead

Receive relevant inquiries while keeping business leads exclusive to the respective supplier.

Gloqr Circle

Connect with businesses, share company updates, and build long-term professional relationships.

THE OUTCOME

A More Connected Way to Do Business

Gloqr provides SMEs with a unified digital presence to showcase their capabilities, manage business inquiries, simplify sales processes, and proactively connect with potential customers and partners.

A comprehensive ecosystem designed to replace fragmented B2B directories with active social commerce.

The platform is designed to support better business visibility, more organized lead management, and new opportunities for collaboration and growth.
